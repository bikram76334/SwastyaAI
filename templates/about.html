About Swashthya AI

SwasthyaAI, a free,
public, no-login web platform that works as a central hub for several medical image screening models, with an interface in English and Nepali.

The platform will host at least eight and up to twelve disease modules, including diabetic retinopathy, multi-disease fundus screening, pneumonia, tuberculosis, skin lesions, brain tumors, malaria and breast cancer imaging. Each module is trained on publicly available data collected from Kaggle .

What it does?
Pick a disease module, upload an image, and get:
  • Predicted class with probabilities
  • Grad-CAM heatmap
  • Uncertainty / low-confidence warning
  • The model's measured performance, including external-dataset results
