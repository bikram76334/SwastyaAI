Grade0

Description

Grade 0 represents Normal Retina or No Apparent Diabetic Retinopathy (NPDR). High blood sugar levels haven't yet caused visible structural changes to retinal blood vessels.

Symptoms
  • No visible retinal lesions, microaneurysms, or hemorrhages.
  • No symptoms
  • Vision is normal
  • No visible retinal changes on examination
Causes
  • Well-managed blood sugar levels
  • Early regular screening in diabetic patients.
Affected Parts

Retinal capillaries, Macula

Recommended Checkup

Annual screening
