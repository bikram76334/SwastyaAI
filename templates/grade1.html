Grade1
  • Overview
Description

The earliest stage of diabetic retinopathy, characterized by tiny bulges in the retinal blood vessels called microaneurysms. Small amounts of fluid or blood may leak into the retina.

Symptoms
  • Usually no symptoms
  • Vision is normal,unaffected
  • Found only on a dilated eye exam or retinal imaging
Causes
  • Initial damage to retinal capillary walls caused by elevated blood glucose levels over time.
  • Chronic hyperglycemia, pericyte loss
Affected Parts

Retinal capillaries, Macula

Recommended Checkup
  • Strictly monitor and control blood sugar, blood pressure, and cholesterol levels.
  • Schedule a comprehensive dilated eye exam every 6 to 12 months.
  • Maintain a balanced diet and regular physical exercise routine.
