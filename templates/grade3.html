Grade3
  • Overview
Description

Commanly known as Severe DR .Many retinal blood vessels become blocked, severely depriving large areas of the retina of blood supply and oxygen. The retina sends signals to body tissues to grow new blood vessels

Symptoms
  • Noticeably blurred, hazy, or fluctuating vision.
  • Increasing number of floaters
  • Reduced visual clarity and difficulty seeing in dim light
Causes
  • Widespread retinal ischemia
  • VEGF overproduction
  • Chronic poor metabolic control
Affected Parts

Widespread retinal capillaries, Retinal tissue (ischemic areas)

Recommended Checkup
  • Schedule frequent specialist follow-ups every 1 to 3 months.
  • Prepare for potential preventive treatments such as Anti-VEGF injections or Panretinal Photocoagulation (laser therapy).
  • Strictly manage cardiovascular risk factors and avoid strenuous activities if advised by a physician.
